2. **Packaging Robustness & Progress Buffer Error**: `Copy-Item assets -Recurse dist/TheWenderer/` lacked `-Force` (failing if a prior `dist/` directory existed), and `Compress-Archive` emitted a `Write-Progress` console buffer error in non-interactive hosts unless `$ProgressPreference = 'SilentlyContinue'` is set.
3. **`NOMINMAX` Redefinition Warnings**: Guarded `#define NOMINMAX` with `#ifndef NOMINMAX` in [VideoPlayer.cpp](file:///d:/GitHub/UITS/CGMLabProject_2DGame/src/VideoPlayer.cpp#L3-L5) and [Audio.cpp](file:///d:/GitHub/UITS/CGMLabProject_2DGame/src/Audio.cpp#L3-L5).
