### AI Thinking & Planning Summary
> **1. Architectural Analysis of the Chunk Deletion & Floating Character Bug**:
> - *Missing Left Chunk Generation*: In [`src/World.cpp`](/src/World.cpp), `World::updateProcedural` was evicting tiles further than 85m away (`std::abs(t.pos.x - playerX) > 85.0f`). When the player progressed rightward, older tiles left of `playerX - 85.0f` were purged, but `leftGenEdge` was never updated (it remained stuck at its initial `-25.0f`). When returning from the Up (Ladder) or Down (Sky-Fall) mini-games back to `platformerSavedPos.x`, any leftward movement (`playerX - 35.0f < leftGenEdge`) failed to trigger chunk generation because `playerX - 35.0f` was still far greater than `-25.0f`.
> - *Floating on Air*: In `returnToPlatformerScene()`, `isGrounded` was set to `!fromJump` without verifying if solid ground existed under the player at `platformerSavedPos.x`. If returning over an ungenerated zone or open chasm, the character remained frozen in mid-air with $v_y = 0.0$ and `isGrounded = true`.
> - *Mathematical & Procedural Resolution*:
>   - In `World::updateProcedural()`, clamp `leftGenEdge = std::max(leftGenEdge, playerX - 80.0f)` and `rightGenEdge = std::min(rightGenEdge, playerX + 80.0f)` upon culling, ensuring generation boundaries strictly match actual tile spans.
>   - Implemented `World::ensureGroundAround(float targetX, EnvironmentTheme theme)` which checks and guarantees continuous chunk coverage for $[targetX - 45.0\text{m}, targetX + 45.0\text{m}]$.
>   - In `returnToPlatformerScene()`, dynamically scan for the highest solid platform tile directly beneath `platformerSavedPos.x`. If found, snap player feet flush to `tileTop + playerHalfH`. If over empty air/chasm, set `isGrounded = false` and initialize downward velocity with active gravity so the player falls naturally onto the safety bedrock/floor rather than floating.
>
