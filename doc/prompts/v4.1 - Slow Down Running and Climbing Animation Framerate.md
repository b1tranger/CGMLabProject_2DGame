### AI Thinking & Planning Summary
> 1. **Root Cause Analysis**:
>    - Investigation of [`/src/main.cpp`](/src/main.cpp) revealed a double-multiplication bug in the sprite animation update logic:
>      - In the physics step, `animTimer` was accumulating with a multiplier: `animTimer += deltaTime * frameRate;` (where `frameRate` was `10.0f`).
>      - In the sprite frame selector during rendering, `frameIndex` applied another multiplier: `static_cast<int>(animTimer * 12.0f) % totalFrames;`.
>      - The compounding multipliers produced an effective playback speed of $10.0 \times 12.0 = 120\,\text{FPS}$ for running ($15$ full run cycles per second) and $8.0 \times 6.0 = 48\,\text{FPS}$ for climbing, creating visual strobe glitching and rapid flickering.
>
> 2. **Framerate Normalization & Calibrated Pacing**:
>    - **Normalized Timer**: Refactored `animTimer` to accumulate pure elapsed real-world seconds: `animTimer += deltaTime;`.
>    - **Running Animation**: Calibrated to $8.0\,\text{FPS}$ ($125\,\text{ms}$ per frame, exactly $1.0\,\text{s}$ per full 8-frame stride). The legs, trailing katana, and robes now animate in a smooth, readable, and fluid rhythm.
>    - **Climbing Animation**: Calibrated to $3.5\,\text{FPS}$ ($285\,\text{ms}$ per step, $\sim 0.57\,\text{s}$ per full left-right hand-over-hand cycle). Climbing ladder tunnels now appears deliberate, tactile, and natural.
>    - **Idle Animation**: Calibrated to $4.0\,\text{FPS}$, providing gentle combat stance breathing without rapid twitching.
