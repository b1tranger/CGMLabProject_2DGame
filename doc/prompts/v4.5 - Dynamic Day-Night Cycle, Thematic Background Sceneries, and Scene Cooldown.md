>    - Built a standalone generator [`build/gen_scenery.py`](/build/gen_scenery.py) using pure Python standard library `struct` and `zlib` to produce four 512x256 seamless looping PNG textures.
> 2. **Day / Night Cycle**:
>    - **Interval**: Cycles every 1.5 minutes (90.0 seconds) of playtime.
>    - **Initial State**: Starts at **Night time** (`dayNightTimer = 0.0f`, `isNight = true`).
>    - **Mathematical Model**:
>      - `cycleIndex = static_cast<int>(dayNightTimer / 90.0f)`.
>      - `isNight = (cycleIndex % 2 == 0)`.
>      - Phase time $t_{\text{phase}} = \text{fmod}(t, 90.0) \in [0.0, 90.0]$.
>      - Smooth 15-second twilight interpolation between 75s and 90s: $s = (t_{\text{phase}} - 75.0) / 15.0 \in [0.0, 1.0]$.
>      - Daylight factor $L \in [0.0, 1.0]$ smoothly transitions from night ($L=0$) to dawn to day ($L=1$) to dusk.
>    - **Celestial & Lighting Integration**:
>      - Dynamic sky gradient: deep midnight indigo (`glClearColor(0.035, 0.05, 0.12, 1.0)`) transitioning through twilight rose-gold (`0.38, 0.16, 0.20, 1.0`) into azure daytime (`0.24, 0.52, 0.85, 1.0`).
>      - 64 twinkling stars and a glowing silver moon with crater halo rendered when $L < 0.75$.
>      - Radiant golden sun with solar corona rendered when $L > 0.25$.
>      - Scenery tint smoothly adapts: moonlight indigo $[0.42, 0.48, 0.70]$ at night, warm sunlight $[1.02, 1.0, 0.98]$ at day, and rose-amber $[1.08, 0.72, 0.58]$ during twilight.
> 3. **15-Second Scene Direction Cooldown**:
>    - When entering `SCENE_LADDER_CLIMB` (Up) or `SCENE_SKY_FALL` (Down), `scenePlayTime` resets to 0.0s.
>    - During the first 15 seconds (`scenePlayTime < 15.0s`):
>      - Autonomous direction switch timer is blocked.
>      - Mystery Fruit Relic pickups award bonus score (+100) and display remaining trial time: `"RELIC +100! (Warp in Xs)"`.
>      - Direction key triggers (`S`/Down or outer edge leap in ladder scene; `W`/Up or Space in sky fall) are guarded, prompting `"SURVIVE 15s FIRST! (Xs left)"`.
>    - At $t = 15.0\text{s}$, a vibrant banner `"=== DIRECTION SHIFTS UNLOCKED! ==="` and corner notification announce trial completion, unlocking all directional transitions.
