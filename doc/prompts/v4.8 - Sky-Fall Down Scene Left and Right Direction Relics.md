### AI Thinking & Planning Summary
> The user requested that the Down direction Sky-Fall diving scene (`SCENE_SKY_FALL`) feature clear, prominent Left and Right direction trigger objects appearing after 15 seconds of free-fall, allowing the player to steer into them to seamlessly resume horizontal running on the regular platformer runner.
>
> **Root Cause Analysis & Challenges in Sky-Fall**:
> 1. In Sky-Fall diving, the player descends at high velocity ($v_y \approx 9.2\,\text{m/s}$). A sparse single item spawning at a random $X$ coordinate was easily bypassed in under $0.5$ seconds if the player was drifting on the opposite side of the screen ($x \in [-7, 7]$).
> 2. The collision radius was previously $1.1\,\text{m}$, requiring precise positioning during high-speed vertical descent.
> 3. Items pre-generated between the player's position and the generation edge ($y_{\text{bottom}} \approx y_{\text{player}} - 25\,\text{m}$) were previously coins and fruits only, leaving a dead zone immediately following the 15-second unlock notification.
>
> **Implementation Strategy**:
> 1. **Immediate Retroactive Conversion & Staggered Gates**:
>    As soon as `scenePlayTime >= SCENE_DIRECTION_COOLDOWN` ($15.0\,\text{s}$) triggers:
>    - Scan all uncollected items ahead of the falling character and immediately convert them into Left (`DIR_LEFT`, Cyan) and Right (`DIR_RIGHT`, Gold) Direction Arrow Relics.
>    - Spawn three staggered multi-lane gates directly in the descent corridor:
>      - Gate 1 ($y - 5.0\,\text{m}$): Left ($x = -2.2$) & Right ($x = +2.2$).
>      - Gate 2 ($y - 10.0\,\text{m}$): Left ($x = -3.0$), Center ($x = 0.0$), & Right ($x = +3.0$).
>      - Gate 3 ($y - 15.0\,\text{m}$): Left ($x = -2.2$) & Right ($x = +2.2$).
> 2. **Continuous Procedural Sky-Fall Streaming**:
>    In the runtime generation loop (`while (skyFallGenBottomY > skyFallPos.y - 25.0f)`), once $t \ge 15.0\,\text{s}$, every descent tier guarantees both a Left and Right direction arrowhead relic, ensuring an unbroken supply of directional exits.
> 3. **Expanded Aerial Pickup Volume**:
>    Expanded the collision volume for `type = 2` relics to $1.85\,\text{m}$ horizontally and $2.1\,\text{m}$ vertically, allowing smooth and responsive steering pickups during high-velocity dives.
> 4. **Warp & Resume Platformer Runner**:
>    Touching an arrowhead relic plays `SFX_RELIC`, sets `platformerSavedDir`, `lastHorizontalDir`, and `currentDirection` to the chosen direction, displays floating text (`"DIRECTION TRIGGERED!\nWARP <<< LEFT"` or `"WARP >>> RIGHT"`), and calls `returnToPlatformerScene(true)` with ground surface raycasting, snapping the player flush to solid ground or continuing natural gravity.
> 5. **Enhanced Visual Presentation**:
>    Enlarged the scale of direction relics in Sky-Fall to $1.65\,\text{m}$ with a sinusoidal breathing pulse ($1.65 + 0.20\sin(5t)$) for high visibility against the dynamic sky.
